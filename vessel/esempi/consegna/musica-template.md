## 🎵 Musica — Lyria / Suno / Udio (regole + template, 3/9/2026)

**⛔ NIENTE TIMESTAMP nel prompt.** I generatori non hanno cronometro: `[00:00-00:06]` è solo testo, il modello
tira dritto fino al suo blocco standard (~2 min). Prompt = **mood + BPM + strumentazione + sezioni**. I secondi si
fanno **in montaggio**. Chiudere con **`[End]`**.
**Il registro lo detta la RECITAZIONE, non il settore:** reel comico su tema industriale ⇒ musica comica.
**120 BPM ⇒ 1 battuta = 2 secondi:** con clip di durata pari ogni stacco cade su una linea di battuta.

### Template collaudato — REEL COMICO (approvato 3/9, reel rientro ferie)
```
[Genre: Quirky Comedy, Playful Industrial, Mockumentary Score]
[Tempo: 120 BPM, light shuffle feel]
[Mood: Comic, Cheeky, Deadpan Irony, Mock-Dramatic Tension, Happy Ending]
[Instrumental]

[Intro]
Pizzicato strings tiptoeing, single woodblock tick, muted tuba note, cheeky and light, like something is about to go wrong.

[Beat Drop]
Bouncy upright bass walking line, brushed snare, staccato bassoon, finger snaps, playful strut with plenty of space.

[Build-up]
Hi-hats enter, pizzicato line climbs step by step, glockenspiel accents, muted trumpet answering, comic optimism building.

[Breakdown - Sudden Silence]
Everything stops dead on one hit, dry room tone, one lonely woodblock tick, awkward comic emptiness.

[Tension Build]
Low staccato strings creeping upward, ratchet and clanking metallic percussion, plucked bass stabs stacking one by one, mock-dramatic pressure, cartoonish suspense, never scary.

[Resolution]
Full warm comic groove, cheerful walking bass, brushed drums, muted trumpet melody returning triumphant, glockenspiel sparkle, sunny and satisfied.

[Bridge]
Groove holds, warmer and rounder, one bright bell accent, friendly and open.

[Outro]
Band pulls back, upright bass walks down two notes, one final soft woodblock tick, clean resolve.

[End]

Instrumental only. No vocals, no vocalizations, no choir, no humming, no spoken word.
Light and low density: this is a comic bed under a male speaking voice — keep the 300 Hz to 3 kHz range clear,
no lead melody in that range. Never epic, never cinematic, never a trailer.
```
- **Troppo cartone animato?** `Quirky Comedy` → `Deadpan Comedy` e via `glockenspiel` da tutte le sezioni.
- **Reel serio/didattico:** stessa struttura, testa sostituita con
  `[Genre: Minimal Industrial Tech, Warm Analog Synth]` + `[Mood: Focused, Clean, Confident]`.
- Generare **2-3 take**, tenere quella con la sezione utile (di solito `Resolution`) più lunga.
- Consegnare sempre insieme al prompt un **foglio tagli** coi secondi del montaggio.
